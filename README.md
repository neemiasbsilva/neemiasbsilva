Hi, I'm an Applied Scientist and **passionate about training multimodal LLMs on large-scale data**. Currently working as an Artificial Intelligence Specialist at [GAB](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-aguia-branca/) and PhD Student at [UTFPR](https://www.utfpr.edu.br/) in [CPGEI](https://www.utfpr.edu.br/cursos/programas-de-pos-graduacao/cpgei-ct), a program rated CAPES grade 7 (Brazil's highest), with a research extension at the University of Toronto. My research spans multimodal LLMs and computational social systems: how vision-language models perceive sentiment, and whether persona prompting produces valid variation in urban perception. I work with [MInDS-Lab](https://github.com/MInDS-lab-UTFPR) at UTFPR and the [Critical Making Lab](https://github.com/CriticalMaking) in Toronto, which hosts [CIFAR-funded](https://cifar.ca/cifarnews/2026/03/28/cifar-awards-over-1m-to-support-sociotechnical-challenges-in-ai-safety/) work on socially grounded AI safety.

More at [neemiasbuceli.dev](https://neemiasbuceli.dev)
